# Hakim Medicine — Compound Formula Reference

This file provides full ingredient breakdowns for classical compound (murakkab) formulas
used in hakim (Unani) medicine. These are multi-ingredient preparations combining simples
according to classical principles of mizaj (temperament) balancing.

Primary sources: Ibn Sina (*Al-Qanun*), Al-Zahrawi (*Al-Tasrif*), *Qarabadin Kabir*
(the classical Unani pharmacopoeia), and Hakim Ajmal Khan's formulary.

## Theriac Formulas

### Theriac Magna (Great Theriac) | Tiryaq Farooq | تریاق فاروق

**Type**: Electuary (ma'jun)
**Primary Indication**: Universal antidote; poisons, animal bites, pestilential fevers
**Temperament of Formula**: Hot 2°, Dry 1° (balanced by cold ingredients)
**Classical Source**: Galen (original), refined by Ibn Sina; standard in all Unani pharmacopoeiae

**Ingredients (classical 64-ingredient formula — key components)**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Viper flesh (roasted, powdered) | 12 dirham | Chief antidotal ingredient |
| Long pepper (*Piper longum*) | 24 dirham | Heating adjuvant |
| Opium (*Papaver somniferum*, dried latex) | 12 dirham | Analgesic, antidote potentiator |
| Squill (*Urginea maritima*, roasted) | 24 dirham | Antidotal, expectorant |
| Galbanum resin | 6 dirham | Antispasmodic |
| Castoreum | 6 dirham | Antispasmodic, nervine |
| Aconite root (islah-processed) | 3 dirham | Antidotal (homeopathic principle) |
| Myrrh (*Commiphora myrrha*) | 12 dirham | Antiseptic, fixative |
| Gentian root | 12 dirham | Bitter tonic |
| Cinnamon (*Cinnamomum zeylanicum*) | 6 dirham | Warming, cardiac tonic |
| Cardamom | 6 dirham | Carminative, aromatic |
| Rose petals (dried) | 12 dirham | Cooling moderant |
| Valerian root | 6 dirham | Nervine, antispasmodic |
| Iris root | 6 dirham | Expectorant |
| Acacia gum | 12 dirham | Binder |
| Honey (clarified) | Sufficient to bind | Vehicle, preservative |
| [+ 48 additional simples per full classical formula] | | |

**Preparation**:
1. Roast viper flesh until dry and powder finely.
2. Process aconite by classical islah (see `herbs.md`).
3. Powder all dry ingredients separately, sieve through fine cloth.
4. Blend all powders uniformly.
5. Warm clarified honey to liquid consistency.
6. Gradually incorporate powders into honey, stirring constantly until smooth electuary forms.
7. Store in sealed clay or glass vessel; matures over 3 months (classical preference: 3–10 years).

**Administration**: 3–6 g dissolved in warm water or wine (historically); taken on empty stomach
or immediately after known poison exposure; 1–3× daily in fever contexts

**Cautions**: ⚠️ Contains opium — classical dose must not be exceeded. Contains processed
aconite and viper components — preparation fidelity critical. Historically contraindicated
in pregnancy. Not for routine use; reserved for serious poisoning or pestilential fever.

## Cardiac Formulas

### Confection of Roses | Ma'jun Gul-e-Surkh / Itriful Saghir | معجون گل سرخ

**Type**: Electuary / confection
**Primary Indication**: Cardiac weakness, palpitations from heat, anxiety, hot temperament
**Temperament of Formula**: Cold 1°, Moist 1° (gently cooling and moistening)
**Classical Source**: Ibn Sina, *Qarabadin Kabir*

**Ingredients**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Dried rose petals (*Rosa damascena*) | 100 g | Chief — cooling cardiac tonic |
| Sandalwood (white, *Santalum album*) | 30 g | Cooling, cardiac, hepatic |
| Camphor | 3 g | Extreme cooling; reduces harārat |
| Lapis lazuli (purified, *lazaward*) | 10 g | Classical cardiac tonic, purifies blood |
| Pearl powder (purified, *marjan*) | 10 g | Cooling, cardiac strengthener |
| Amber (*anbar*) | 5 g | Cardiac and cerebral tonic |
| Violet flowers (dried) | 20 g | Cooling, moistening |
| Purslane seed | 10 g | Cooling diuretic |
| Quince seed mucilage | 20 g | Soothing, binding |
| Rosewater | 30 ml | Solvent, aromatic |
| Honey (white, clarified) | To bind | Vehicle |

**Preparation**:
1. Powder all dry ingredients finely; sieve.
2. Warm honey gently; add rosewater.
3. Incorporate powders gradually; blend to smooth paste.
4. Store in sealed glass container.

**Administration**: 6–10 g dissolved in cold rosewater or plain cold water;
taken on empty stomach in morning; 1–2× daily

**Cautions**: Contains camphor — do not exceed formula dose. Lapis lazuli requires
classical purification (calcination and washing) before use. Contraindicated in
cold-temperament patients without warming adjuvants.

## Purgative Formulas

### Compound Colocynth Pill | Hab Shiyaf / Hab Qinnah | حب شیاف

**Type**: Pill (hab)
**Primary Indication**: Severe constipation, hepatic congestion, black bile excess,
humoral imbalance requiring strong purgation
**Temperament of Formula**: Hot 2°, Dry 3°
**Classical Source**: Al-Zahrawi, Ibn Sina

**Ingredients**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Colocynth pulp (*Citrullus colocynthis*) | 10 g | Chief purgative |
| Aloe (Socotrine, dried) | 10 g | Laxative, hepatic |
| Scammony resin (*Convolvulus scammonia*) | 5 g | Powerful purgative |
| Mastic (*Pistacia lentiscus*) | 5 g | Gastric protectant |
| Ginger (dried) | 3 g | Warming adjuvant |
| Cardamom | 2 g | Carminative, prevents cramping |
| Rose petals | 5 g | Moderating adjuvant |
| Gum arabic | Sufficient | Binder |
| Rosewater | Sufficient | Wetting agent for pill mass |

**Preparation**:
1. Powder colocynth, aloe, scammony, mastic, ginger, cardamom, rose separately; sieve.
2. Dissolve gum arabic in rosewater to form mucilage.
3. Blend all powders; add mucilage gradually; knead into firm pill mass.
4. Roll into pills of 3 g each; dry in shade; coat with silver leaf (optional, classical).

**Administration**: 1–2 pills (3–6 g) at night with warm water; effect expected morning

**Cautions**: ⚠️ Colocynth and scammony are powerful irritant purgatives. Historically
contraindicated in pregnancy, hemorrhoids, inflammatory bowel conditions, and elderly
with weak constitution. Mastic is included specifically to reduce GI irritation. Overdose
causes violent purgation and cramping — classical texts warn to start with 1 pill.

## Antispasmodic Formulas

### Harad Compound | Ma'jun Harad Kabir | معجون ہلیلہ کبیر

**Type**: Electuary
**Primary Indication**: Epilepsy, convulsions, hysteria, melancholy, black bile disorders
**Temperament**: Hot 2°, Dry 1°
**Classical Source**: Ibn Sina, *Qarabadin*

**Ingredients**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Black myrobalan (*Terminalia chebula*) | 50 g | Chief: brain and nerve tonic |
| Yellow myrobalan (*Terminalia citrina*) | 30 g | Phlegm expellant, brain tonic |
| Emblic myrobalan (*Phyllanthus emblica*) | 30 g | Rasayana tonic, cooling |
| Beleric myrobalan (*Terminalia bellirica*) | 20 g | Phlegm, respiratory |
| Asafoetida (*Ferula assa-foetida*) | 10 g | Antispasmodic, carminative |
| Castoreum (dried) | 6 g | Antispasmodic, uterine (classical) |
| Valerian root | 15 g | Nervine sedative |
| Peony root | 10 g | Antiepileptic (classical) |
| Long pepper | 10 g | Adjuvant, warming |
| Dry ginger | 10 g | Warming, aids absorption |
| Nutmeg | 5 g | Narcotic-nervine (small dose) |
| Honey | To bind | Vehicle |

**Preparation**:
1. Powder all ingredients separately; combine and sieve.
2. Blend into warm honey; prepare electuary.
3. Mature 7 days before use (classical recommendation).

**Administration**: 6–12 g with warm water or warm milk before sleep; 1× daily

**Cautions**: Contains castoreum and asafoetida — avoid in pregnancy.
Nutmeg in excess is toxic; formula dose is safe. Avoid with hot-temperament without
cooling adjuvants.

## Wound & Surgical Preparations

### Black Plaster | Marham Siyah | مرہم سیاہ

**Type**: Ointment / plaster
**Primary Indication**: Deep wounds, abscesses, drawing out foreign bodies,
chronic ulcers, gangrenous edges
**Classical Source**: Al-Zahrawi (*Al-Tasrif*, Surgical Book)

**Ingredients**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Yellow wax (beeswax) | 100 g | Base |
| Lard (purified) or olive oil | 50 g | Emollient base |
| Litharge (lead oxide, *martak*) | 20 g | Classical desiccant, antiseptic |
| Pine resin (*qatran*) | 20 g | Antiseptic, drawing agent |
| Myrrh (*murr*) | 10 g | Antiseptic, granulation promoter |
| Frankincense (*kundur*) | 10 g | Antiseptic, tissue-strengthening |
| Copper sulfate (burnt, *zaj*) | 5 g | Cauterizing, antiseptic |
| Verdigris (*zanjar*) | 5 g | Antiseptic, escharotic |

**Preparation**:
1. Melt beeswax and lard/oil together over low heat.
2. Add litharge and stir continuously until incorporated.
3. Add pine resin; stir until uniform.
4. Off heat: add powdered myrrh, frankincense, copper sulfate, verdigris.
5. Stir until cooled into uniform ointment. Store in clay pot.

**Administration**: Apply to wound on linen cloth; change dressing 1–2× daily.
Use until wound granulates.

**Cautions**: ⚠️ Contains lead (litharge), copper sulfate, and verdigris —
all historically standard wound-care agents; significant modern toxicity concerns
with systemic absorption from large wounds. Historical documentation only.
Al-Zahrawi specifies this for wounds that resist simple treatment.

## Tonic Formulas

### Chyawanprash-Equivalent | Ma'jun Muqawwi | معجون مقوّی

**Type**: Electuary
**Primary Indication**: General debility, aging, convalescence, reproductive weakness,
lung weakness, immune strengthening
**Temperament**: Hot 1°, Moist 1° (gently heating and moistening — ideal tonic balance)

**Ingredients**:

| Ingredient | Classical Qty | Role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| Emblic myrobalan (*amla*, *Phyllanthus emblica*) | 100 g | Chief rasayana / muqawwi |
| Long pepper | 10 g | Adjuvant |
| Dry ginger | 10 g | Adjuvant |
| Black pepper | 5 g | Adjuvant |
| Cinnamon | 10 g | Cardiac tonic, warming |
| Cardamom | 10 g | Carminative, aromatic |
| Saffron (*zafran*) | 2 g | Cardiac, liver tonic, mood |
| Ashwagandha root | 30 g | Nervine tonic, reproductive |
| Sesame seeds (black) | 20 g | Nutritive tonic |
| Purified ghee | 50 g | Anupana (vehicle) |
| Honey | 150 g | Vehicle and tonic |
| Sugar candy (*tabasheer* type) | 30 g | Cooling moderant |

**Preparation**:
1. Cook amla in water; remove seeds; dry and powder.
2. Powder all dry ingredients; blend.
3. Warm ghee; add honey; blend.
4. Incorporate all powders into warm ghee-honey base.
5. Add saffron dissolved in a few drops of rosewater last.
6. Store in glass or ceramic container.

**Administration**: 6–12 g in morning on empty stomach, with warm milk; daily

**Cautions**: Generally safe; avoid in acute fever; excess honey/ghee may not suit
diabetic or obese constitution; adjust with physician guidance in classical framework.
