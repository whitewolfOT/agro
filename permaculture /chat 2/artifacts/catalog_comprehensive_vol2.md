## ORL — Oral, Dental & Gum Disorders (أمراض الفم والأسنان — Amrāḍ al-Fam wa al-Asnān)

*Humoral basis*: Dental pain arises from bilious heat, phlegmatic cold, or worms in teeth.
Gum disease from excess phlegm and blood. Mouth ulcers from excess heat.
Ibn Sina devotes Book III, Part 1, chapters on the mouth to these conditions in detail.

## EAR — Ear Disorders & Deafness (أمراض الأذن — Amrāḍ al-Udhn)

*Humoral basis*: Ear pain from excess phlegm or putrid matter lodged in the ear canal.
Hearing loss from cold, thick phlegm obstructing the passages, or from humors ascending from stomach.
Ibn Sina treats ear disorders extensively in *Al-Qanun*, Book III, Chapter on the Ear.

## GOT — Gout (نقرس — Nuqris)

*Humoral basis*: Gout is the descent of corrupt humors — primarily black bile mixed with phlegm — into the joints of the feet and hands. Cold, wet seasons increase susceptibility.
Ibn Sina dedicates specific chapters to *nuqris* as distinct from general arthritis.

## AGE — Aging, Longevity & Anti-Senescence (حفظ الصحة للشيخوخة — Ḥifẓ al-Ṣiḥḥa lil-Shuyūkha)

*Classical doctrine*: Ibn Sina opens *Al-Qanun* Book I with a detailed theory of aging as the progressive drying and cooling of the body's innate moisture (*ruṭūbat gharīziyya*) and natural heat (*ḥarārat gharīziyya*). Longevity medicine (*ṭibb al-mashāyikh*) aims to slow this process through diet, lifestyle, and specific restoratives.
