# Hakim Medicine — Master Catalog
## Complete Reference: 41 Ailment Categories · ~220 Verified Ingredients

**Primary source**: Ibn Sina, *Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb* (Bulaq ed. 1877) — Arabic base throughout.
**Secondary**: Al-Biruni *Kitab al-Saydana*; Al-Zahrawi *Al-Tasrif*; Ibn Rushd *Kitab al-Kulliyyat*;
Dioscorides (Arabic trans.); Ibn al-Baytar *Jami' al-Mufradat*; Hakim Sharif Khan *Ilaj al-Amraz*;
Qarabadin Kabir; Bakhtiar English trans. 1999 (sense-check only).
**Latin Venice eds. 1595/1608**: historical cross-check only — never primary source.

**Conventions**
- Temperament notation: Hot/Cold N°, Moist/Dry N° (1–4 scale per Ibn Sina)
- ⚠ = toxic; islah/detoxification mandatory before internal use where stated
- Doses in classical units: 1 dirham ≈ 3 g; 1 ratti ≈ 125 mg
- All entries are historical documentation. Not a modern prescription.
