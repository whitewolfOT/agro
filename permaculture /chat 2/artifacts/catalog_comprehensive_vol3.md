### POX — Smallpox & Pox Diseases (الجدري — Al-Jadarī)

*Primary source*: Ibn Sina, *Al-Qanun* Bk. IV; Al-Razi (*Rhazes*), *Kitab al-Jadari wa al-Ḥaṣba*
(the first monograph on smallpox and measles — the most important classical text for this category).
Al-Razi distinguishes smallpox (*jadarī*) from measles (*ḥaṣba*) with clinical precision.

*Humoral basis*: Smallpox caused by fermentation and putrefaction of residual fetal blood
in the body. Pustules are the body's attempt to expel this corrupt matter outward through
the skin. Treatment: support this expulsion while preventing complications.

### VEN — Venereal Disease & Syphilis (أمراض المباشرة — Amrāḍ al-Mubāshara)

*Note*: Classical hakim sources do not have a precise pre-modern equivalent of "syphilis"
as identified after 1493 CE. However, Al-Zahrawi, Hakim Sharif Khan, and the *Qarabadin*
describe a class of chronic ulcerating, contagious disease transmitted by sexual contact —
referred to as *athar al-rās* (سرطان الباه — diseases of intimate contact) — for which
mercury preparations became the standard classical Unani treatment by the post-medieval period.

### HIC — Hiccup & Belching (الفواق — Al-Fawwāq)

*Humoral basis*: Hiccup arises from sudden spasm of the diaphragm caused by irritant vapors
from the stomach (from cold food, acidic bile, or excess wind). Ibn Sina in *Qanun* Bk. III
distinguishes hot-type (*fawwāq ḥārr*) from cold-type (*fawwāq bārid*) and prescribes
different remedies for each.

| 2 | Fennel distillate | عرق الشمار — 'arq al-shamār | Hot 2°, Dry 1° | Distillate drunk warm | Oral | Wind-type hiccup; carminative; settles diaphragmatic spasm from gas | 30–60 ml warm distillate. Source: Qanun Bk. III; Dioscorides Arabic |

| 5 | Asafoetida | حلتيت — ḥiltīt | Hot 3°, Dry 2° | Small dose dissolved in warm water | Oral | Hiccup from retained gas and wind; disperses flatulent vapor | 0.3 g dissolved in warm water. Source: Qanun Bk. III |
| 6 | Mint (spearmint) | نعناع — na'nā' | Hot 2°, Dry 2° | Strong infusion drunk warm | Oral | Wind-type and phlegmatic hiccup; carminative; settles stomach | 5 g strong infusion immediately. Source: Qanun Bk. III; Dioscorides Arabic |
